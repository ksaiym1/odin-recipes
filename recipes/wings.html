Lemon Pepper Wings

Image Credit: Rawpixel

Description

These lemon pepper wings are a great variation of the ever-popular tailgating and sports-bar snack. They are very simple and easy to make. They are sure to become a favorite when watching the next game.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups peanut oil, or as needed
  • 0.25 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons lemon pepper seasoning
  • 6 pearl onions
  • 16 chicken wings

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a deep fryer or large saucepan at 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Whisk butter and lemon pepper seasoning together in a large bowl.
  3. Place onions in the preheated oil and cook until just beginning to brown, about 5 minutes. Remove and discard onions.
  4. Working in batches, cook wings in oil until crispy on the outside and cooked through, about 8 minutes. Remove wings to a paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil.
  5. Toss wings with lemon pepper mixture until completely coated. Return to paper towel-lined plate to drain excess liquid.
